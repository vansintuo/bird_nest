Our story

Three generations, one cave system

Our farmers have worked the same limestone caves outside Battambang since the 1970s, learning which chambers the swiftlets favor each season and when a nest is ready to take without disturbing the colony.

We built this company to shorten the distance between that cave and your kitchen — cutting out the resellers who bleach nests for a whiter, heavier product, and paying farmers fairly for slower, cleaner work.

Our process

Five steps between cave and jar

Nothing is rushed and nothing is hidden — this is the same sequence we'd walk you through in person.

01

Harvest

Taken by hand after chicks have fledged, never before.

02

Sort

Graded by strand length, colour and cave of origin.

03

Clean

Feathers removed with water and tweezers — no chemicals.

04

Test

Each batch checked for nitrite levels and purity.

05

Package

Sealed and dated within 48 hours of cleaning.

Every batch is nitrite-tested and certified for export before it leaves Battambang.
